Awaab's Law

New UK legislation on damp and mould — what landlords need to know

Awaab's Law is UK legislation aimed at ensuring the safety of social housing by requiring landlords to address damp and mould hazards promptly. It's named after Awaab Ishak, a two-year-old who tragically died from mould exposure in 2020 — a case that highlighted the urgent need for stricter regulations. The law came into force on 27 October 2025.

Investigate & Fix

Landlords must investigate and fix damp and mould within 10 working days.

Repairs

Necessary repairs must be completed within 5 working days.

Emergencies

Emergency hazards must be responded to within 24 hours.

Written Summary

Landlords must provide a written summary of investigation findings within 3 working days.

The law aims to prevent the structural damage and serious health risks associated with damp and mould, ensuring safer living conditions for tenants. Warrington Damp Proof supports landlords and homeowners in meeting these standards with thorough damp surveys and prompt, lasting remedial work.
